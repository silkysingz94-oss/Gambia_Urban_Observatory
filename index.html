Gambia Urban Observatory

An interactive geospatial platform for exploring population distribution, settlement structure, urban growth patterns and spatial indicators across The Gambia. Load administrative boundaries, Enumeration Area GeoJSON and statistical tables, then compare geographic units through thematic mapping, indicator summaries, rankings and charts. Select an area on the map or use the administrative selector to focus the analysis. All calculations are based on the data currently loaded into the dashboard.

Data sources

Administrative boundaries: geoBoundaries / OCHA COD-AB where applicable.

Buildings, roads and places: OpenStreetMap contributors.

Population estimates: user-supplied census data, WorldPop or GHSL datasets when separately obtained and loaded.

Official statistics: Gambia Bureau of Statistics publications and approved data sources.
